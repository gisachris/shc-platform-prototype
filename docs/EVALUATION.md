# Evaluation Instrument (TAM / UTAUT)

Supporting Objective 5 of the research proposal: evaluate performance, usability, and effectiveness of the Smart Hybrid Conference Management Platform (RCB case study).

Use after a guided pilot demo. Target respondents: organizers, attendees, speakers, IT staff (aligned with proposal sample categories).

## Scoring

Likert 1–5 (1 = Strongly disagree, 5 = Strongly agree). Compute means and percentages \(P = F/N \times 100\).

## A. Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)

1. Using this platform would improve my ability to manage / participate in hybrid conferences. (PU)
2. The platform makes registration, scheduling, and session access easier than disconnected tools. (PU)
3. Learning to use the platform is easy for me. (PEOU)
4. I find the interface clear for my role (attendee / organizer / speaker). (PEOU)
5. I intend to use a platform like this for future Rwanda conferences. (Intention)

## B. UTAUT

6. The platform helps me accomplish conference tasks more quickly. (Performance Expectancy)
7. Interacting with the system requires little mental effort. (Effort Expectancy)
8. People whose opinions I value would encourage use of this system. (Social Influence)
9. I have the devices and connectivity needed to use the platform. (Facilitating Conditions)

## C. DeLone & McLean (system success)

10. The system is reliable during registration and live sessions. (System Quality)
11. Conference information (schedule, tickets, tourism) is accurate and up to date. (Information Quality)
12. Support / help content is adequate for first-time users. (Service Quality)
13. I am satisfied with the overall experience. (User Satisfaction)
14. The platform provides net benefits versus email/spreadsheet workflows. (Net Benefits)

## D. Feature-specific (proposal coverage)

15. Hybrid / LiveKit participation is usable for remote attendees.
16. Engagement tools (Q&A, polls) improve session interaction.
17. Networking suggestions / messaging help me find relevant contacts.
18. Organizer analytics/exports support decision-making.
19. Tourism information is useful for planning a Rwanda stay.

## Open questions

- What was the most valuable feature?
- What blocked you or caused confusion?
- What should be prioritized for future work?

## Analysis notes

Present results with frequency tables, bar charts, and thematic coding of open responses. Relate findings back to dependent variables in the conceptual framework: efficiency, accessibility, engagement, networking, satisfaction.
